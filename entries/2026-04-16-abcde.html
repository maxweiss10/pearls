ABCDE Approach to the Unwell Patient

Start at the end of the bedogram

AssessIntervene
A Airway: color; are they talking to you? Airway concern → call for help STAT: RICU
B Breathing: RR, O₂ sats + O₂ requirement; WOB; talking in full sentences; lung sounds O₂: NC, NRB, HFNC, NIV. CXR. VBG/ABG. Call RT
C Circulation: HR, BP; CRT; warm/cold; JVP; heart sounds; edema; UOP EKG; reliable access; labs: POCT glucose, CBC, BMP, LFT, lactate, coags (blood cultures, troponin, d-dimer, type & screen); fluid / blood product / pressors / inotropes / antibiotics
D Disability: blood glucose; GCS; pupils; moving all extremities Code Stroke. STAT CT. ?Narcan
E Everything else: temp; examine everything else; detailed hx + chart dig
