Cases

  • Procedure, intervention, therapies? IV v. well-planned PO
  • With nausea & vomiting? IV
  • Worsening pain? Move down the ladder / adjust current meds
Building Blocks
  • Start mild
  • Empower PO >> IV: reassurance, treat nausea, PO lasts longer
  • Don't forget non-pharm! Repositioning, heat, ice, warm tea, reassurance, modify room
