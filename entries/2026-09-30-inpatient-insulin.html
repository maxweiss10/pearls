Patient on Insulin Who Is Eating
  • In general: continue the patient's normal outpatient insulin regimen
  • Do not stop the outpatient insulin and use a sliding scale
  • Poorly controlled outpatient glucoses on current regimen → consider starting a more appropriate insulin regimen
  • Good control → cut back to 70%
  • To prevent hypoglycemia: for most type 2 patients on insulin, cut back to 70–80% of outpatient dose; very high dose of glargine → may consider 50%

Tube Feeds
  • New method: just start the SQ autotitration order for patients NPO / on TPN / on enteral feeds; it titrates the insulin to what is required automatically
  • Method 1: take the last 24 h insulin infusion; basal: 24 h total / 2; aspart: 24 h total / 10, given q4h
  • Method 2: similar to method 1, just using a higher proportion of basal insulin
  • Method 3: if no IV insulin, just use 1 U per 6–10 g CHO to start, then calculate as per method 1
