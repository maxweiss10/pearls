Potassium
Target
≥3.5 (≥4 if CAD/arrhythmia)
Rule
10 mEq ↑K 0.1, recheck q40 mEq
Route
PO > IV. Fix Mg first
Caution
HD: only if <3.0
PO
KCl IR powder ≥20 mEq q4–6h (ER pills giant)
IV
10 mEq/h peripheral, 20 mEq/h central on tele
Magnesium
Target
≥1.7 (≥2 if CAD/arrhythmia)
Rule
2 g ↑Mg 0.5
Route
IV > PO. Low Mg → low K, low Ca
PO
Mg oxide 400 mg TID ×1d
IV
Mg sulfate 2 g
Phosphorus
Target
Replete if <2
Route
PO preferred. IV if <1, or <1.5 + sx (weak, MSK pain, resp failure, CHF, Δ MS)
Caution
IV phos binds Ca → hypoCa
CKD: halve dose
PO
Phos-NaK or K-Phos Neutral 1 packet QIDNaK 7 K / 7 Na, Neutral 1 K / 13 Na mEq
IV
15–45 mmol over 6 h as K-Phos (1.5 mEq K/mmol) or Na-Phos (1.3 mEq Na/mmol)
Calcium
Target
Replete if sx, long QTc, Ca <7.5, iCa <1.15
Rule
1 g gluconate ↑Ca 0.5
Route
PO > IV unless iCa <1.0
Caution
Correct for albumin & phos first. Tolerate low iCa in TLS (Ca×Phos precipitates)
PO
Ca carbonate 1250 mg BID
IV
Ca gluconate 1–2 g
Code
CaCl₂ 0.5–1 g q2–5min
