Decongestion — first 24 h
1. Set the target — 2–3 L net neg/day (sick, very overloaded) · 1–2 L (frail, mild)
2. Pick the dose — 2–2.5× total home PO dose, given IV
3. Reassess at 4 h — poor UOP = failed dose → double it, redose same day
4. Replete lytes aggressively — recheck often while titrating
Not working — block more nephron
1. Max the loop — double, then double again
2. Add thiazide-type — metolazone 5 mg PO · chlorothiazide 500 mg IV
3. Add acetazolamide — 500 mg IV daily
4. Loop infusion — bumetanide 0.5–2 mg/hr · furosemide 5–20 mg/hr — bolus first
Afterload reduction — choosing a vasodilator

Afterload ≠ BP. Unloading ↑ SV — BP can improve.
