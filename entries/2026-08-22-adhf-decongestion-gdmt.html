Diuresis dosing
1. Target — 2–3 L net neg/day (sick / very overloaded) · 1–2 L (frail / mild)
2. Dose — 2–2.5× total home PO dose, given IV
3. Reassess 4 h — poor UOP = failed → double, redose today
4. Replete lytes aggressively — check often while titrating
Stalled — block more nephron
1. Max the loop — double, double again
2. Thiazide-type — metolazone 5 mg PO · chlorothiazide 500 mg IV
3. Acetazolamide — 500 mg IV daily
4. Loop gtt — bumetanide 0.5–2 mg/hr · furosemide 5–20 mg/hr — bolus first
Afterload reduction

Afterload ≠ BP. Unloading ↑SV — BP can improve.

PO short Hydralazine q8h + ISDN TID — the PO nitroprusside
PO long ARNI (or ARB)
IV arterial Nitroprusside — cyanide limits duration · nicardipine / clevidipine — HTN emergency only
IV venous NTG — preload > afterload · tachyphylaxis · pick for ACS / CAD
Avoid IV hydralazine — unpredictable · dilt / verapamil — neg inotropes, harm in HFrEF
