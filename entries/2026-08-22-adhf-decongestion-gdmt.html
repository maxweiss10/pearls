CardiologyADHF — Decongestion & GDMT
Decongestion — First 24 h
  • Set the target: 2–3 L net neg/day (sick, very overloaded); 1–2 L (frail, mild)
  • Pick the dose: 2–2.5× total home PO dose, given IV
  • Reassess at 4 h: poor UOP = failed dose → double it, redose same day
  • Replete lytes aggressively: recheck often while titrating
Not Working — Block More Nephron
  • Max the loop: double, then double again
  • Add thiazide-type: metolazone 5 mg PO; chlorothiazide 500 mg IV
  • Add acetazolamide: 500 mg IV daily
  • Loop infusion: bumetanide 0.5–2 mg/hr; furosemide 5–20 mg/hr — bolus first
Afterload Reduction — Choosing a Vasodilator

Afterload ≠ BP. Unloading ↑ SV, so BP can improve.

Sequencing & Discharge
  • Start order: SGLT2i + MRA → ACEi / ARB / ARNI → β-blocker (once decongesting)
  • By discharge: all four on low dose, titrate as outpatient; f/u 1 wk
  • No ARNI? Losartan daily or valsartan BID, or hydralazine + ISDN
