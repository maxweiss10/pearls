Calcium albumin correction is not real.

Design
Population-based cross-sectional study, Alberta. 22,658 adults with total calcium and ionized calcium drawn at the same time, 2013–2019, across inpatient, ED and outpatient settings.
Finding
Agreement with ionized calcium, the reference standard:
Unadjusted total Ca 74.5% Simplified Payne 63.0% Original Payne 58.7%
Adjusting made agreement worse, not better.
Why
Misclassification was highest exactly where the formula gets reached for: albumin <30 g/L.the hypoalbuminemic patient is the whole reason anyone corrects
So
Use the unadjusted total calcium as the practical alternative, or measure an ionized calcium when it actually matters.
Erratum (JAMA Netw Open 2025) corrects only an eGFR unit label in Results and Table 1; the agreement figures above are unchanged.
