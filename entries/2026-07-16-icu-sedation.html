Critical CareSedation, Analgesia & Paralysis
Sedation
Drug / DosePearls
Propofol (first line)
TGs 400–500		 Quick on/off. Hypotension. PRIS: acidosis, rhabdo, bradycardia, green urine
Dexmedetomidine (Precedex)
0.2–1.4 mcg/kg/hr		 Bradycardia, hypotension. Agitated delirium. Less respiratory depression
Midazolam (Versed)
2–20 mg/hr		 Neutral BP. Builds in fat stores. ICU delirium, tolerance
Ketamine
≤25 mg/hr		 Emergence reaction. Transient ↑HR/BP. Sedation + analgesia. SVR preserved
Analgesia

Consider bowel regimen + methadone taper

  • Fentanyl: 25–200 mcg/hr; neutral BP
  • Dilaudid (hydromorphone): hepatic; hypotension; good for opioid tolerant pts
  • Morphine: renal; hypotension; anxiolytic, air hunger; comfort care
Paralysis
  • Monitoring:
    • ACURASYS (mortality)
    • ROSE
    • V/V (sedated)
    • TOF (“train of four”)
    • BIS
  • ICU myopathy is the cost
  • Cisatracurium (Nimbex): 0.15–0.20 mg/kg/hr
  • Rocuronium (Zemuron): 0.6 mg/kg. Vecuronium (Norcuron)
