Twelve-panel echo reference grid covering the imaging and Doppler findings used to support RV dysfunction / cardiac tamponade physiology. Structural/pericardial signs (A-C): pericardial effusion with >5mm fluid in diastole, RV free wall thickening >5mm, and a plethoric IVC >21mm with <50% inspiratory collapse (suggests elevated RA pressure). Tricuspid and RV systolic function (D-G, I-J): elevated TR peak velocity, reduced TAPSE, RV dilation (RVEDD/LVEDD >1.0, basal diameter >41mm), reduced RV fractional area change, reduced tricuspid annular S' by DTI, and reduced RV free wall longitudinal strain. Loading/interdependence (H): septal shift and D-shaped LV distinguishing RV pressure- vs volume-overload patterns. Global RV performance (K-L): elevated RIMP (Tei index) by DTI and reduced 3D RV ejection fraction. Useful as a one-page checklist when working up RV failure, pulmonary hypertension, or suspected tamponade physiology on echo.
