Reading the chart
  • Both entry gates must pass: supraventricular rhythm with a P wave before each QRS, and QRS ≥120 ms onset to offset in the widest lead. Paced, VT, or 110–119 ms stops you here
  • Read only the terminal 40 ms in V1 and ask which side of baseline it ends on. Max is always RIGHT: ends up in V1 = RBBB — ends up, not starts up
  • Terminal deflection positive → RBBB: rsR′, notched R, qR, or a plain R. Negative → LBBB: QS or rS, a small r is allowed
  • Confirm in I and V6. RBBB wants an S wider than R or >40 ms, and a septal q is fine there. LBBB wants a broad monophasic R with no q in I, aVL, V5 or V6 — a q refutes it
  • Both halves are required, not either. If either half fails, call it IVCD: nonspecific conduction delay, not bundle branch block
