How to read the chart
  1. Both entry gates must pass: a supraventricular rhythm with a P wave before each QRS, and QRS ≥120 ms measured onset to offset in the widest lead. Paced, VT, or 110–119 ms stops you here.
  2. Read only the terminal 40 ms in V1 and ask which side of baseline it ends on. Max is always RIGHT: ends up in V1 = RBBB. Ends up, not starts up.
  3. Terminal deflection positive → RBBB (rsR′, notched R, qR, or a plain R). Negative → LBBB (QS or rS; a small r is allowed).
  4. Confirm in I and V6. RBBB wants an S wider than R or >40 ms, and a septal q is fine there. LBBB wants a broad monophasic R with no q in I, aVL, V5 or V6 — a q refutes it.
  5. Both halves are required, not either. If either half fails, call it IVCD: nonspecific conduction delay, not bundle branch block.
