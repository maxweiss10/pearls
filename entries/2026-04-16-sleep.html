Sleep in the Hospital
  • ↓ Interruptions: vitals, alarms, blood draws, meds (diuretics, overnight admin), noisy neighbors
  • Night is night — promote sleep: make a bedtime, TV + lights off, blankets, eye masks, ear plugs, warm milk or tea
  • Day is day: lights on, blinds up, ↓ naps, OOB / mobilize, eating!
  • EVERYONE: Melatonin @1800
