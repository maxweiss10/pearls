Inspectionvarus, valgus or neutral

Palpation of joint linespalpating distal femur, proximal tibia
Examine for quad atrophypatient straightens legs, compare side to side

Palpation of patellar facets
Evaluate for effusion

McMurray test (meniscus)
Bonus
Lachman (ACL)
Valgus stress (MCL)
Varus stress (LCL)
Posterior drawer (PCL)
4. Standing
Squat and Thessaly tests (meniscus)
