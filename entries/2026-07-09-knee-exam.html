To identify patellofemoral pain, OA and meniscus tears

1 · Standing — inspection (varus, valgus or neutral)
2 · Seated — palpation of joint lines (palpating distal femur, proximal tibia) · examine for quad atrophy (patient straightens legs, compare side to side)
3 · Supine — palpation of patellar facets · evaluate for effusion · range of motion: flexion, extension · McMurray test (meniscus)
Bonus · Supine — Lachman (ACL) · valgus stress (MCL) · varus stress (LCL) · posterior drawer (PCL)
4 · Standing — squat and Thessaly tests (meniscus)
