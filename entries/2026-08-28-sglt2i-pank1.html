Forelli et al. · Science · 8/2026
SGLT2 inhibitors directly bind and activate pantothenate kinase 1 (PANK1), the rate-limiting enzyme of CoA synthesis — an off-target action that improves cardiac fuel use and contractility.
Mechanism
1. SGLT2i — bind PANK1 at physiologic concentrations (conformational change; site modeled in silico, confirmed by mutagenesis)
2. PANK1 activity ↑ — rate-limiting step of CoA synthesis
3. CoA ↑ — broad increase in fuel use across human cardiac tissue (stable isotope infusion)
4. Contractility ↑ — PANK activation necessary and sufficient in human cardiomyocytes
Why it matters
SGLT2 is not expressed in the heart, and the mortality benefit holds with or without diabetes — PANK1 is the first target that accounts for it.
