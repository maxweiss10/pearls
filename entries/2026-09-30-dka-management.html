* After H&P: capillary glucose + serum or urine ketones. Begin 1 L NS (or buffered crystalloid, eg LR) over 1 h. STAT: ABG (or mixed venous gas), CBC w/ diff, UA, glucose, BUN, lytes, chemistry, Cr. ECG; if needed, CXR and bacterial cultures.

¶ Corrected Na: add 2 mEq to Na for each 100 mg/dL of glucose above 100.

Δ Alternative to fixed-rate infusion: variable-rate infusion per nurse-driven protocol.

DKA criteria: hyperglycemia (eg, new-onset diabetes: glucose ≥200); venous pH <7.3; HCO₃ <18 mEq/L; ketonuria ≥2+ or BOHB ≥3 mmol/L. Lab normals vary: check local ranges. Monovalent ions: 1 mEq/L = 1 mmol/L. Glucose mg/dL to mmol/L: 200 = 11.1; 250 = 13.9; 150–200 = 8.3–11.1.
