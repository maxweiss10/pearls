Reading the full algorithm (open image)
  • Top box: initial evaluation, then the four columns branch off it and run together
  • Column 1, IV fluids: volume status (severe / mild / cardiogenic shock) → correct the deficit within 24 h → fluid by corrected Na → dextrose once glucose <250
  • Column 2, potassium: kidney function first, then three K bands (<3.5 / 3.5–5 / >5)
  • Column 3, insulin: IV for moderate–severe, SUBQ for uncomplicated; both feed the same titration box and the glucose <250 step
  • Column 4, bicarbonate: pH <7.0 vs ≥7.0
  • Bottom boxes: fluids and insulin columns both end in the identical monitoring and SUBQ-transition box
  • Footnote marks: * initial labs and studies; ¶ corrected-Na formula; Δ a variable-rate nurse-driven drip may replace the fixed 0.1 U/kg/h
