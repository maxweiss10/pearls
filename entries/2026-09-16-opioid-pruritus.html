Mechanism and First Look (White Book p. 154)
  • Mechanism: central μ-receptor effect, not histamine (exception: morphine releases histamine → urticaria/flushing). Benadryl is ineffective unless there is a rash or true allergic reaction
  • First look: confirm itch without rash (rash/hives/wheeze → treat as drug reaction, not opioid pruritus)
    • Check pain control, sedation, RR
    • Note which opioid and route (neuraxial and morphine are the usual culprits)
Overnight Sequence
#StepDoseNotes
1 Nalbuphine (Nubain)
mixed κ-agonist / μ-antagonist		 5 mg IV q6h prn
(2.5 mg if elderly or frail)		 White Book first-line; reverses itch, preserves analgesia. Repeat once at 30–60 min if needed. Can precipitate withdrawal in high-dose or chronic opioid users
2 Ondansetron (Zofran)
5-HT3RA		 4 mg IV Safe add-on, usually already on the MAR. Best evidence with neuraxial opioids, modest for systemic. Check QTc
3 Rotate or reduce
e.g. morphine → hydromorphone or fentanyl		 ↓ 25–50% on rotation Side effects with pain relief → rotate with dose reduction. Side effects without pain relief → rotate IV opioid, no reduction. Morphine is the histamine offender, prefer hydromorphone/fentanyl
4 Naloxone gtt
low dose, sub-reversal		 0.25 mcg/kg/hr
(up to 1 mcg/kg/hr)		 Severe or refractory. Compatible for simultaneous administration with morphine; needs pump and order set, best for PCA patients. Caution with high-dose ER opioids; watch for pain recurrence
5 Antihistamine
sedative benefit only		 Hydroxyzine 25 mg PO
or diphenhydramine
12.5–25 mg PO		 Last resort if the goal is sleep. Avoid IV Benadryl in favor of PO. Sedation stacks with the opioid
Skip Overnight
  • Propofol: subhypnotic 10–20 mg, brief effect
  • Naltrexone PO: neuraxial prophylaxis only, shortens analgesia
  • Mirtazapine / gabapentin: chronic or palliative settings
Chronic / Sickle Cell
  • Outpatient: non-sedating antihistamine or 5-HT3RA
  • Inpatient: naloxone gtt 0.25 mcg/kg/hr alongside morphine or PCA
Handoff
  • Flag for day team: scheduled nalbuphine prn, opioid rotation, or pall care if refractory
