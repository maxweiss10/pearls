Reading the ladder by ejection fraction
  1. Four agents run across every EF band: SGLT2 inhibitor, MRA and loop diuretic (Class I), plus vaccination (IIa). Start there whatever the number.
  2. At EF ≤50% add ACE-I / ARNI / ARB and a beta-blocker (Class I), and IV iron for iron deficiency (I/IIa).
  3. Below that the bands narrow as the evidence narrows: vericiguat at ≤45% for high-risk HFrEF with recent worsening (IIb); hydralazine/ISDN for self-identified Black patients and digoxin or digitoxin for symptoms and fewer hospitalizations at ≤40% (IIa); ivabradine at ≤35% for symptomatic HFrEF in sinus rhythm with HR ≥70 (IIa).
  4. Going the other way, EF ≥45% opens semaglutide or tirzepatide in obesity with HFpEF (IIa), and ≥50% adds ACE-I / ARNI / ARB in selected HFpEF patients (IIb).
